Loan / EMI Calculator

Monthly EMI, total interest and total payment for any loan.

Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments. Results are estimates and may differ from a lender’s fees or rounding.

Worked example

For a 10,000 loan at 8% annually over 5 years, r is the monthly rate and n is 60 monthly payments.

How this calculator works

Monthly EMI, total interest and total payment for any loan. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so the values you enter are not sent to a server.

Using the result

Check that your inputs use the intended units and assumptions. Round only the final result so intermediate rounding does not introduce avoidable error.
