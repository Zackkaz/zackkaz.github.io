Age Calculator

Your exact age in years and days.

Formula

Approximate age = elapsed days ÷ 365.25The decimal-year figure is an estimate based on 365.25 days per year; the total elapsed days is also shown.

Worked example

The calculator measures elapsed time from the birth date to today and reports years and total days.

How this calculator works

Your exact age in years and days. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so the values you enter are not sent to a server.

Using the result

Check that your inputs use the intended units and assumptions. Round only the final result so intermediate rounding does not introduce avoidable error.
