Tip Calculator

Split a bill and calculate the tip per person.

Formula

Tip = bill × (tip percentage ÷ 100); per person = (bill + tip) ÷ peopleEnter the pre-tip bill amount, desired tip percentage and number of people. The calculator shows the tip, total and equal share per person.

Worked example

A 15% tip on a 50 bill is 7.50. Split between two people, the total is 28.75 each.

How this calculator works

Split a bill and calculate the tip per person. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so the values you enter are not sent to a server.

Using the result

Check that your inputs use the intended units and assumptions. Round only the final result so intermediate rounding does not introduce avoidable error.
