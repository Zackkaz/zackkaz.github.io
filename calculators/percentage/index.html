Percentage Calculator

Find what a percentage of a number is, instantly.

Formula

Result = (percentage ÷ 100) × numberPercent means “per hundred.” Convert the percentage to a decimal by dividing by 100, then multiply by the original number.

Worked example

10% of 200 = (10 ÷ 100) × 200 = 20.

How this calculator works

Find what a percentage of a number is, instantly. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so the values you enter are not sent to a server.

Using the result

Check that your inputs use the intended units and assumptions. Round only the final result so intermediate rounding does not introduce avoidable error.
