Date Difference Calculator

Days and years between two dates.

Formula

Difference in days = end date − start dateThe result uses elapsed calendar days. The year estimate uses 365.25 days to account for leap years over long periods.

Worked example

From 1 January to 11 January is 10 elapsed days.

How this calculator works

Days and years between two dates. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so the values you enter are not sent to a server.

Using the result

Check that your inputs use the intended units and assumptions. Round only the final result so intermediate rounding does not introduce avoidable error.
