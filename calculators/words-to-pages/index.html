Words to Pages Calculator

Estimate pages from a word count.

Formula

Estimated pages = word count ÷ words per pageActual page count depends on font, spacing, margins, headings and images. Change the words-per-page value to match your document format.

Worked example

2,500 words at 500 words per page is approximately 5 pages.

How this calculator works

Estimate pages from a word count. The calculation runs entirely in your browser, so the values you enter are not sent to a server.

Using the result

Check that your inputs use the intended units and assumptions. Round only the final result so intermediate rounding does not introduce avoidable error.
